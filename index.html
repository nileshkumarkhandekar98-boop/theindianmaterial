Your host

Nileshd Khandekar, a designer on the making side

I trained in lifestyle accessory design and have spent my career where beautiful objects are actually made: with suppliers, workshops and materials, across India and the UAE.

I have developed premium gifting in Abu Dhabi, merchandised lighting, marble and leather in Mumbai, and founded a materials venture that turned agricultural waste into biocomposites. That is why the conversations on the show go past the finished piece, to the material, the maker and the decisions behind it. I research every guest and plan each conversation myself.
